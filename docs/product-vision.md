# FinPilot AI

## Vision

FinPilot AI is an AI-powered personal finance assistant that helps users understand their spending habits, manage budgets, achieve savings goals, and receive personalized financial insights while maintaining strong privacy and security.

The goal is to provide users with an intelligent financial companion instead of just another expense tracker.

---

## Problem Statement

Most finance applications only display transactions and charts.

Users still struggle to answer questions like:

- Where did my salary go?
- Am I overspending?
- Can I afford a new purchase?
- Which subscriptions are wasting money?
- How can I save more?

FinPilot AI solves this by combining AI, memory, budgeting, and analytics.

---

## Target Users

- Students
- Working Professionals
- Freelancers
- Small Business Owners

## Core Features

- Authentication
- CSV Import

- AI Chat
- Long-term Memory
- Budget Planning
